TypeWrite sounds: where they come from
======================================

Every .wav in this folder is cut from a real typewriter recording.
Tools/make_sounds.py downloads the recordings and rebuilds these files;
it holds the exact cut points.

Changes made to the recordings: mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz,
rumble below 40 Hz removed, lightly de-noised (by at most 12 dB, using the
recording's own quiet stretch), cut into single events, faded in and out,
and set to a common loudness. The bell is also high-passed at 3 kHz to
remove a keystroke under it, and the return has its pauses shortened.
