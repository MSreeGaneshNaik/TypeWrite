    TypeWrite for Mac and iPhone

    The same machine as a native app for your Mac, and for your iPhone with real haptics. Paste this into Terminal on a Mac:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MSreeGaneshNaik/TypeWrite/main/install.sh | bash
    1. It builds TypeWrite with Xcode, which is free in the Mac App Store, and puts it in your Applications folder.
    2. Plug in your iPhone first to install it there too, signed with your own Apple ID (a free one works).
    3. Paste the same line again any time. It updates when there's something new.

    Source and the full guide: github.com/MSreeGaneshNaik/TypeWrite
